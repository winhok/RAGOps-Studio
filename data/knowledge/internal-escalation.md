# Internal Support Escalation Playbook

This document is restricted to support and admin roles.

Escalate to Tier 2 when a customer reports three or more failed payment attempts with different valid payment methods, when a refund remains pending for more than 10 business days after provider submission, or when a carrier investigation is unresolved for more than 5 business days.

For a suspected account takeover involving a changed payout destination, mark the case as high priority and route it to Trust & Safety immediately.

Agents must include the order or account identifier, timeline, actions already taken, and relevant trace or provider reference IDs in the escalation note.
