# Northstar Commerce Shipping Guide

Standard domestic shipping usually arrives in 3 to 5 business days after fulfillment. Expedited shipping usually arrives in 1 to 2 business days after fulfillment.

Orders may take up to 24 hours to enter fulfillment after payment is confirmed. Tracking becomes available when the carrier scans the parcel. If tracking has not updated for more than 72 hours, support should open a carrier investigation.

Customers may change the delivery address only before the order enters fulfillment. Once an order is fulfilled, support must not promise an address change; the customer may contact the carrier if the carrier offers rerouting.

A package is treated as lost when the carrier confirms loss or when there has been no tracking movement for 7 calendar days after the expected delivery date. Support may then offer a replacement or refund after verifying the order.
