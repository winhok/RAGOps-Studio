# Northstar Plus Subscription

Northstar Plus renews monthly on the customer's billing date. Customers can cancel at any time from Account > Billing. Cancellation stops the next renewal but does not automatically refund the current billing period.

If a customer was charged after a cancellation request, support should verify the cancellation timestamp and billing timestamp. If cancellation was completed before the billing event, the charge should be escalated for billing correction.

Plan upgrades take effect immediately and may generate a prorated charge. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.

Subscription billing information is visible only to authenticated customers and support agents with the billing role.
