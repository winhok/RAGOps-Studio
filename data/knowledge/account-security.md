# Account Security and Access

Support must never ask customers to share passwords, one-time passcodes, recovery codes, or full payment-card numbers.

For suspected account takeover, support should first secure the account by invalidating active sessions and triggering the verified recovery flow. Changes to primary email or phone number require identity verification.

A customer can request deletion of their account from Settings > Privacy. Deletion enters a 7-day grace period before permanent processing. During the grace period the customer can cancel the deletion request by signing in and confirming account recovery.

Support agents may view account status, recent security events, and verification status, but they must not expose internal risk scores to customers.
