# Northstar Commerce Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 30 calendar days of delivery for unused physical products in resalable condition. Refunds are returned to the original payment method after the warehouse confirms the returned item.

Digital gift cards and personalized products are not refundable unless required by law. Shipping fees are not refundable when the original shipment was delivered correctly.

For damaged or incorrect items, customers should contact support within 7 days of delivery. Support may issue a replacement or refund after validating the order and evidence.

Refund status can be checked after a return is received. Most approved refunds are submitted to the payment provider within 2 business days, and banks may take an additional 3 to 7 business days to post the credit.
